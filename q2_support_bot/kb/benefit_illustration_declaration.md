## What the Benefit Illustration Shows
The benefit illustration provided to you by HDFC Life in connection with the above referenced Application/Proposal Form Number ("Benefit Illustration") is intended to show year-wise premiums payable and benefits under the policy.

## Guaranteed and Variable Benefits
Please take note that some benefits are guaranteed and some benefits are variable with returns based on the future performance of your insurer carrying on life insurance business. If your policy offers guaranteed benefits then these will be clearly marked "guaranteed" in the Benefit Illustration table. If your policy offers variable benefits then the Benefit Illustration will show two different rates of assumed future investment returns, of 8% p.a. and 4% p.a. These assumed rates of return are not guaranteed and they are not the upper or lower limits of what you might get back, as the value of your policy is dependent on a number of factors including future investment performance.

## Note on Annualized Premium
Annualized Premium excludes underwriting extra premium, frequency loadings on premiums, the premiums paid towards the riders, if any, and Goods & Service Tax.
