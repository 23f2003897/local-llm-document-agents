# Multiple Policies Consent

_Source file: split.jpeg (HDFC Life Split & Multiple Policies - Customer Consent Form)_

## Purpose of the Consent Form
This form is signed when a customer has taken multiple policies from HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited in the last six months. The customer confirms the reason for buying more than one policy and accepts that splitting cover across policies may cost more.

## Customer Confirmation (filled in)
Proposer / Life Assured Name: Ashok
I Mr. / Mrs. / Miss. Ashok hereby confirm that I have taken multiple policies from HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited in last six months.

## Reason for Buying Multiple Policies
Options on the form (tick one):
- Financial Planning (viz. payout on different life stages, different payment terms, etc.) - TICKED on this form
- Different investment / protection needs (viz. protection, child plan, etc.)
- Separate policy purchased for different beneficiary
- Others: Please specify (blank)
