## What an Assignment Is
An assignment transfers the policyholder's (assignor's) rights, title and interest in a life insurance policy to another person or institution (the assignee), for example to a bank as security for a loan.

Example of a condition: Assignor will not have the right to execute any policy alteration or processing without the written consent of the Assignee. ## Important Notes on Assignment
1. The assignment of a policy shall automatically cancel any nomination made in the policy except assignment in favour of HDFC Life Insurance Company.
2. This assignment shall not be effectual unless this Assignment Form is duly completed and delivered, accompanied by the original Policy bond to the company. If the Assignee is a major, then please submit the ID proof and address proof of the Assignee.
3. For cases where the assignment is in favour of an individual or financial institution apart from our channel partners, then the Assignor must get the signature of the Authorised Signatory on the company seal, stamped on any one corner of the Endorsement Sheet.
4. Appointee Signature will be required in cases where the Assignee is a minor.
5. Policy servicing charges may be levied as applicable. Please refer to your Policy document for details.
6. Taxes will be deducted at source, if applicable, from the payments made under a life insurance policy in accordance with the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Tax laws are subject to change from time to time.
